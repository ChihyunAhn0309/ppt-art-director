## S04 — 가상 예시: 최적화 B의 지연 40% 감소

**Purpose:** demonstrate how to make a quantitative comparison traceable while clearly identifying illustrative evidence.

**Exact visible copy:**

- Title: 가상 예시: 최적화 B의 지연 40% 감소
- Chart label: 평균 지연 (ms) · 낮을수록 좋음
- Categories and values: 기준선 100, 최적화 A 78, 최적화 B 60
- Focal number: −40%
- Calculation: 100 → 60 ms / (100 − 60) ÷ 100
- Caveat: 가상 수치이며 오차 범위는 설정하지 않음

**Evidence:** values intentionally constructed for a design/tool test; no empirical source or study is claimed. Reduction = (100 − 60) / 100 = 0.40. Do not describe the difference as statistically significant.

**Visual:** native column chart in approximately two-thirds of the content width; value axis starts at zero and ends at 120 ms; direct values above the bars. Right column holds the reduction and calculation. Use the Research Ink palette; main bars blue, result emphasis dark teal. All text remains native. Keep the caveat visible.

**Speaker notes:** explain that lower latency alone does not determine the best configuration; the next slide compares accuracy loss. All numbers are illustrative.

**Motion:** static. The comparison is already simple and should be visible as a whole.

**Feedback example:** “B를 65 ms로 바꾸고 감소율도 고쳐줘.”

**Required propagation:** B bar and value label 60 → 65; title and focal number 40% → 35%; calculation becomes (100 − 65) / 100; update the native chart data, the following comparison table, source ledger, and notes. Keep unrelated palette, layout, and accepted copy unchanged.
