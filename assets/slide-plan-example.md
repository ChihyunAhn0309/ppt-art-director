## S04 — Illustrative result: B reduces latency by 40%

**Purpose:** make the quantitative comparison traceable and identify the evidence as fictional.

**Exact visible copy:**

- Title: Illustrative result: B reduces latency by 40%
- Chart label: Mean latency (ms) · lower is better
- Categories and values: baseline 100, optimization A 78, optimization B 60
- Focal number: −40%
- Calculation: 100 → 60 ms / (100 − 60) ÷ 100
- Caveat: Illustrative values; uncertainty is unspecified
- Intended fit: title one line at the chosen presentation size; caveat one line. Rewrite or rebalance before shrinking below a readable size.

**Evidence:** values constructed for a design/tool test. Reduction = (100 − 60) / 100 = 0.40. No empirical source or statistical significance is claimed.

**Visual:** editable column chart in two-thirds of the content width, a zero-based value axis ending at 120 ms and direct values above bars. Right column holds the reduction and calculation. Use Research Ink with blue bars and a dark teal result. Keep text native and the caveat visible.

**Notes:** lower latency alone does not determine the best configuration; the next slide compares accuracy loss. All numbers are illustrative.

**Motion:** static; the comparison should be visible as a whole.

**Feedback:** “Change B to 65 ms and correct the reduction.”

**Propagation:** update the B bar and label 60 → 65; title and focal number 40% → 35%; calculation (100 − 65) / 100; native chart data, following table, source ledger and notes. Preserve unrelated accepted choices.
